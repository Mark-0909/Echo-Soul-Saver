**Echo: Soul Saver** is a 2D rogue-like action-platformer developed in **Godot Engine (GDScript)**. 

Players navigate a perilous ascent through corrupted dungeon floors by fluidly shifting between two polar states: **Human** and **Ghost**. Balancing the tactical benefits of physical combat against the high-risk, life-draining powers of the spectral plane is key to surviving every run.

---

## ⚔️ Core Mechanics

* **Dual-Form Transformation**: Seamlessly swap between physical Human and spectral Ghost states.
  * 🗡️ **Human Form**: Direct physical combat, standard platforming gravity, vulnerable to physical traps.
  * 👻 **Ghost Form**: Phasing mobility, soul absorption, and bypass mechanics balanced by **continuous life-drain decay**.
* **Soul-Gathering Economy**: Harvest essence from defeated enemies and environmental rifts to fuel powers, restore vitality, and unlock run upgrades.
* **Monster Combat AI**: Adaptive enemy behaviors featuring distinct aggro ranges, patrol paths, and telegraphed attack patterns.
* **Floor Ascension Progression**: Procedural floor-to-floor rogue-like progression where each cleared tier introduces higher hazard density and elite mob variants.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🔁 **High-Stakes Risk/Reward Loop** — Life drains while ghosted; manage transformation timing to avoid self-inflicted death.
* 💀 **Rogue-like Replayability** — Escalating floor difficulty and procedural encounter distribution.
* 🎨 **Atmospheric 2D Pixel Art** — Handcrafted animations distinguishing physical strikes from ethereal particle effects.
* ⚙️ **Responsive Kinematics** — Tight platformer physics tuned for dodge rolls, jump arcs, and mid-air state shifts.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Engine**: [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org/)
* **Scripting**: GDScript
* **Physics & Systems**: Custom 2D kinematic controllers, state machines, and finite AI state systems
* **Visuals**: 2D Pixel Art, tilemaps, and frame-by-frame sprite animation
